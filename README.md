## Projects

### 1. Analyzing Students' Mental Health

**Technologies:** PostgreSQL, SQL, Python, pandas, Matplotlib  
**Completed:** October 2026

Exploratory analysis of 201 international university students examining associations between length of stay in Japan and depression (PHQ-9), social connectedness (SCS), and acculturative stress (ASISS).

The project includes SQL-based data validation and aggregation, Python visualizations, interpretation of findings, and a discussion of methodological limitations.
